Exam Management Redefined

Catholicate College brings you an intelligent ecosystem to automate and streamline examinations for a conflict-free academic experience.

What is this System?

A digital solution engineered to eliminate manual orchestration and optimize institution resources during high-pressure examination periods.

The Examination Orchestration System is a BCA final year project designed for Catholicate College. It replaces tedious manual exam management with an intelligent automated framework. By leveraging technology, we ensure accurate, conflict-free, and transparent operations for all stakeholders—Administrators, Faculty, and Students.
  • Eliminates scheduling conflicts between courses.
  • Automates randomized seating arrangements.
  • Simplifies invigilator duty assignments.
  • Provides secure digital dashboards for real-time data.

Core Capabilities

Automated Logic

Advanced randomization algorithms that handle seating and scheduling without human intervention, minimizing manual errors.

Secure Access

Multi-layered authentication ensuring data confidentiality and integrity for all institution stakeholders.

Real-time Updates

Instant notifications and updates regarding timetables, hall allocations, and duties directly to your dashboard.

Examination Guidelines

Identity Check

Valid college ID and Hall Ticket are mandatory for entry into the exam zone. No documentation, no entry.

Prohibited Items

Smartwatches, mobile phones, and any Bluetooth-enabled devices are strictly prohibited inside the hall.

Punctuality

Candidates must report to their assigned hall at least 20 minutes before the session starts. Late entry is disallowed.
